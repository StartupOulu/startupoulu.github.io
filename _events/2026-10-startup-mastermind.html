Pitch your current challenge, get insights from BusinessOulu experts and solve problems together with peers. description: | The Startup Mastermind Club is a peer-support network for startup teams and founders that have started their journey. Pitch your startup and discuss your current challenges, gain insights from BusinessOulu experts, and take part in collective problem-solving with peers.

🔹 Want to solve a problem? Prepare a 5-minute pitch of your startup and your current challenge.
🔹 Just want to listen in? You're welcome to join, contribute to the discussion and learn from others' experiences.

The club is open for all, but especially tailored for founders and teams who have moved beyond the idea stage – for example alumni of the Startup Express Pre-Incubator, Founder's Corner at Business Corner or the OYSTER program. ---
