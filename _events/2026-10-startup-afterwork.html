The October Startup Afterwork is here! 🍻

Everyone is welcome: founders, investors, startup-minded people and anyone curious about the startup world. All you need is the will to meet new people, swap ideas and enjoy a relaxed evening in good company.

No preparation, no pitching, no need to figure out in advance who to talk to. Come as you are, grab a drink and join in. 🙌
