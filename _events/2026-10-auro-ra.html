Applications close 11.10. description: | AURO.RA Fall 2026 is a 5-day intensive startup sprint by OuluES, running from Wednesday evening 14.10. to Sunday evening 18.10.

This edition is built around one core idea: customer → product. Instead of building first and looking for customers later, you'll spend the first days talking to real people and uncovering real problems – and only then start building. So what you ship at the end is something people actually want.

The program ends with a HackerHouse 🏡, where teams live and build together for the final two days before pitching on Sunday evening.

Teams of 1–3. All backgrounds welcome – you don't need to know how to code, you need to care about solving a real problem.

Applications close 11 October. ---
